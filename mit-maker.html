Demo

{%- if p.demo == 'reip' %}

This replays two of my hardware trials side by side. In both, the leader robot is given a fault partway through that makes it send the team the wrong way. With REIP, the other robots notice, vote the faulty leader out, and keep exploring. Raft, the standard method I compared against, has no way to notice.

Each side is that method's median run out of five, replayed from the robots' own logs.

{%- elsif p.demo == 'linqbot' %}

Your webcam takes the place of the AR glasses. An open hand moves the arm, a pinch closes the claw, and a fist freezes the arm so you can reposition your hand. The video stays in your browser.

Built by me using Claude Code for our hackathon demo presentation; Jerry Li later ported it to his LinqBot write-up.
